Frequently Asked Questions

Some helpful information about HanyaGo.

HanyaGo is a transport-booking platform concept designed to help travellers plan journeys and access available transport options.
HanyaGo can save authenticated booking requests. A saved request is not a confirmed ticket: an operator must still confirm availability, driver assignment and fare.
Online payment has not been connected yet. A payment provider such as Paystack or Flutterwave must be integrated securely before real payments can be accepted.
Authenticated booking requests are stored in the HanyaGo database. Passengers should use their account to view their own booking details.
