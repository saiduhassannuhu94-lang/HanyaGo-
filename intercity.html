Built for clearer journeys

Route, seats and payment in one flow

Intercity coaches, local shared rides and on-demand trips have different booking rules. HanyaGo keeps them separate.

⌕

1. Search live departures

Search town-to-town and date. When a route is not listed, the page says so instead of fabricating a timetable.

▦

2. Choose exact seats

Seat labels and availability come from a database inventory, and availability is checked again when you reserve.

✓

3. Pay, then verify

A successful redirect alone is not proof of payment. HanyaGo verifies transactions through Paystack.

For local pickup, private rides or Keke Napep, visit local journey planner or Keke fare catalogue.
