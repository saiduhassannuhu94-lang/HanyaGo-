Help centre

Questions, answered clearly.

Useful answers about booking requests and what HanyaGo can currently do.

HanyaGo keeps local ride requests separate from scheduled intercity coach trips. Use the local planner for flexible pickup journeys and the intercity page to search published departures and choose numbered seats.
For local rides, sign in and submit a request; it stays pending until the operator confirms availability, driver/vehicle and fare. For scheduled intercity travel, open Intercity Seats to search published departures.
The route/date has no matching departure published in HanyaGo right now. It does not prove that no buses operate on that road. Try another date or contact support; do not assume an unlisted service has a guaranteed departure, price or seat.
A local ride request is not a confirmed ticket until the operator confirms it. An intercity booking becomes a confirmed e-ticket only after Paystack verifies payment and HanyaGo confirms the seat allocation.
Paystack checkout is available only when the booking is eligible. Local ride fares must be confirmed by an operator first. For intercity trips, a seat is held temporarily and becomes booked only after payment verification. A screenshot or browser redirect alone is not proof.
Google and GitHub sign-in work only when their respective providers and OAuth credentials are correctly configured in Supabase. Email sign-in also requires the hCaptcha Site Key when CAPTCHA protection is enabled.
Self-service booking changes and cancellation are not connected yet. Contact HanyaGo with your booking reference before making travel arrangements.
Live driver visibility depends on an approved driver being online and sharing their location. A blank live map does not mean a driver is nearby, and location sharing should be used only with permission.
The Contact page shows the support channels and message form. The official HanyaGo support email and WhatsApp number must be configured before messages can be delivered through those channels.
