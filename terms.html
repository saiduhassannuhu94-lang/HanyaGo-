Terms of Service

These terms describe the current HanyaGo website and booking-request service.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Current service status

HanyaGo currently provides transport information and a way for authenticated users to submit journey requests. Submitting a request does not, by itself, reserve a vehicle or seat, appoint a driver, confirm a departure or create a confirmed ticket.

2. Booking requests and confirmation

After a request is saved, its status may remain pending while availability, route, pickup arrangements, driver or vehicle assignment, and the agreed fare are reviewed. Do not travel on the assumption that your request is confirmed. Wait for explicit confirmation from an authorised HanyaGo representative or transport operator.

3. Prices and payments

Local ride checkout is allowed only after an authorised operator confirms availability and a payable fare. Scheduled intercity trips show a fare entered by operations for a reviewed departure; selected seats are held temporarily and are not confirmed until Paystack verifies the exact payment and HanyaGo confirms the seat allocation. A browser redirect, transfer screenshot or booking reference alone is not proof of payment. Never share card details, passwords or one-time codes in support messages. If Paystack reports successful payment but a ticket does not become confirmed, do not pay again; contact support with the payment reference.

4. Accounts and accurate information

Provide accurate contact and journey details, protect your password, and notify HanyaGo if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission. Do not impersonate another person, attempt to access another passenger’s data, abuse the service, or submit misleading or unlawful content.

5. Changes, cancellations and delays

Self-service changes and cancellation workflows are not yet available on the website. If you need to correct a request or ask about a delay, contact HanyaGo and include your booking reference. Any changes or cancellations must be explicitly agreed with the responsible operator.

6. Availability and third-party services

Transport availability depends on independent operators and actual operating conditions. Local ride requests remain pending until an operator confirms details. The intercity search lists only scheduled departures published and staff-reviewed through HanyaGo operations; a route suggestion is not a promise of a daily service. HanyaGo does not guarantee that an unlisted vehicle or timetable exists, and HanyaGo staff review is not a government licence or certification. The website may use external services for hosting, authentication, payment processing, security checks and photographs, which are governed by their respective terms.

7. Website availability and updates

We may update the site, correct errors, or temporarily suspend features for maintenance or security. We do not guarantee that every feature will always be available or free from interruption.

8. Contact and questions

Use the Contact page for support when the published contact channel is configured. Never include passwords, one-time codes or sensitive payment information in a support request.

Important: These website terms are a practical starting point for the current service, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review before commercial launch.
