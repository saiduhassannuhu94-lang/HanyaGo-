Clear steps, fewer surprises

From journey idea to booking request.

HanyaGo is being built around a straightforward travel flow that keeps the booking status honest.

1

Enter your journey

Provide your pickup point, destination, travel date and preferred transport option. Check the details before submitting.

2

Sign in or create an account

An account is required before the current booking flow can save your request. Keep access to your account email and password.

3

Submit the booking request

HanyaGo saves an authenticated request and returns a booking reference. At this stage, the request is marked pending.

4

Wait for confirmation

A human operator must still confirm transport availability, driver or vehicle assignment, pickup arrangements and the agreed fare.

What is not live yet: live vehicle inventory, real-time trip tracking, automated fare quotes, online payment and automated operator confirmation are not currently connected. HanyaGo will not describe a pending request as a confirmed ticket.
