Clear steps, fewer surprises

Local rides and intercity seats are different bookings.

HanyaGo keeps the two journeys separate so you can see what is requested, what is reserved and what is actually confirmed.

Choose your travel type

Start in the right place

Do not search a scheduled coach ticket in the flexible local-ride form. They need different details and confirmation rules.

Local / on-demand

Pickup to destination

For Keke Napep, local private cars, taxis, shuttles and flexible journeys. Enter pickup, destination, date, time and passenger count.

Request a local ride →

Your request stays pending until an operator confirms availability, vehicle/driver and payable fare.

Scheduled intercity

Town to town, seat by seat

For coaches or scheduled shuttles leaving known terminals. Search both towns and the travel date, compare published departures and choose exact seat numbers.

Find intercity seats →

Only departures published by operations appear. A quick-search route does not promise service on that day.

Flow 01

Local ride request

1

Enter the journey

Set pickup, destination, preferred date/time, passenger count and a reachable phone number. If an address is not listed, type it clearly with a nearby landmark.

2

Sign in and submit

HanyaGo saves the authenticated request and returns a booking reference. A reference means the request was recorded, not that a vehicle or driver is already assigned.

3

Wait for operator confirmation

The operator must confirm service availability, pickup instructions, assignment and fare. Paystack checkout must not start before the booking is confirmed with a valid payable amount.

4

Pay through verified checkout

Where checkout is enabled, use HanyaGo’s Paystack flow. Check booking status after returning. Do not trust screenshots, transfer messages or a browser redirect alone.

Flow 02

Intercity scheduled seat ticket

1

Search route and date

Choose origin state/town, destination state/town and departure date. HanyaGo searches operator-published departures. If none are listed, it must not invent a time or price.

2

Compare the actual departure

Check operator name, terminal at each end, departure time, vehicle, fare per seat and number of seats available. Read luggage and amenity notes.

3

Choose exact numbered seats

Available seats can be selected, while held or booked seats are unavailable. HanyaGo checks the database again before reserving to reduce double booking.

4

Enter passenger details

Book for yourself or someone else. For another passenger, choose the relationship; if it is not listed, select Other and write it. Confirm name and contact number carefully.

5

Hold, pay and retrieve the e-ticket

Seats are held for 15 minutes while checkout is completed. Paystack must verify payment and HanyaGo must confirm seat allocation. The printable ticket QR opens a live status check without exposing the passenger’s phone number.

Honest availability status: No intercity departures have been published to HanyaGo’s database yet. This means the online schedule is not populated; it does not mean buses never operate on these roads. Operations must enter a real operator, terminal, date/time, fare and physical seat layout before a trip appears. The checkout and ticket code is deployed to Supabase, but a successful end-to-end ticket transaction still needs controlled testing before public launch.
