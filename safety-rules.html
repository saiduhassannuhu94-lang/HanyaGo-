Passenger & Driver Safety Rules

These rules apply to every HanyaGo transport category, including Keke Napep, private car, bus/shared ride, shuttle and other services listed on the platform. Respect, accurate details and clear confirmation help protect everyone.

Service status: A booking request or reference is not a confirmed trip. Wait until an authorised operator confirms the driver/vehicle, pickup, route, fare and travel time. Do not treat a profile, badge or uploaded document as a guarantee of safety.
01

Rules for passengers

  • Use your real name and a reachable phone number, and provide accurate pickup, destination and passenger-count details.
  • Check the vehicle registration, driver identity and trip details before entering. If details do not match the confirmed booking, do not board.
  • Agree the fare and payment method before departure. Never assume a request is confirmed or pay someone whose identity and authority cannot be verified.
  • Wear a seat belt where provided, use the required safety equipment, and do not pressure a driver to overload a vehicle or break traffic laws.
  • Treat drivers respectfully. No threats, harassment, violence, discrimination, unwanted touching or damage to property.
  • Share live location only when you choose to and only for the trip purpose. Stop sharing when it is no longer needed; do not post another person’s location publicly.
  • Keep valuables secure, tell a trusted person about your trip when appropriate, and report suspicious behaviour or safety concerns promptly.
02

Rules for drivers and operators

  • Provide truthful identity, contact details, current licence and vehicle/operating documents required by law and HanyaGo review.
  • Keep your profile and phone number accurate. Do not use another person’s account, impersonate a driver or lend your account to an unapproved person.
  • Only switch your “live/available” status on when ready to accept trips. Share precise GPS location only while on duty and as permitted by the service.
  • Confirm the passenger, destination, pickup point, fare and payment method before starting. Never change the agreed fare without explaining it and getting the passenger’s agreement.
  • Do not drive under the influence, while dangerously tired, while using a handheld phone, or in an unsafe or unroadworthy vehicle.
  • Never overload, speed dangerously, threaten, harass, discriminate against, detain or unlawfully detour a passenger.
  • Keep passenger phone numbers, photos, documents and location private. Do not save, copy, publish or reuse them beyond legitimate trip and legal needs.
  • Report crashes, threats, suspected fraud, lost property and serious incidents through the available support channel and, where necessary, to emergency services.
03

Identity checks and documents

  • HanyaGo may request relevant evidence depending on the user’s role, such as a student ID and school name, employee or business credentials, or a driver’s licence and vehicle documents.
  • Submit only genuine documents that belong to you or that you are authorised to provide. Do not upload passwords, bank PINs or one-time verification codes.
  • Identity documents should be restricted to authorised reviewers and must not be displayed on public profiles. Review or approval does not guarantee a person’s conduct or establish a criminal background check.
  • Do not publish someone’s ID, address, phone number, face or live location to accuse or shame them.
04

Reporting threats or suspicious activity

  • If you are in immediate danger, move to a safer public place if possible and contact the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement service.
  • Keep the booking reference, time, route and relevant messages. Share evidence privately with the authorised support team or appropriate authorities, not as a public accusation.
  • Do not label a person “wanted”, a kidnapper, terrorist or criminal based only on a name, photograph, rumour, anonymous upload or resemblance.
  • Any serious allegation must be checked by authorised humans against reliable, traceable official information, with context and a way to correct mistakes. HanyaGo must not present an unverified accusation as fact.
  • False reports, threats, retaliation, harassment and discriminatory reporting are prohibited.
05

Live location, privacy and respectful conduct

Precise location, phone numbers, profile photos and identity documents can expose people to harm if misused. Access should be limited to the people and purposes that genuinely need it. A passenger’s trip location should only be shared with the assigned driver/operator when the passenger has enabled sharing; a driver’s live location should only be available while they are actively online and subject to the service’s privacy controls. Never track, threaten, stalk or contact another user outside the legitimate booking purpose.

Before the tripCheck the booking details, identity, vehicle and agreed fare.
During the tripRespect boundaries, follow traffic law and use location sharing carefully.
After the tripEnd unnecessary sharing and report any concern through a private channel.

Need help with a trip?

Keep your booking reference and contact HanyaGo support. If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, do not wait for a platform response; contact local emergency services.
