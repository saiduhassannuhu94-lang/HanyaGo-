Your information matters

Privacy Policy

This notice explains how information is handled by the current HanyaGo website and booking-request flow.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Information that may be collected

If you create an account, the authentication service processes the email address and password you provide, along with any profile details you submit such as your name and phone number. If you submit a booking request, HanyaGo stores details such as your pickup location, destination, travel date, transport option, booking status and a booking reference. Do not enter information that is not needed to arrange your journey.

2. How information is used

Account and booking information is used to authenticate users, create and review booking requests, maintain booking records, help resolve support enquiries and protect the service against misuse. Contact-form entries are not stored by this static website. Once the official support email is configured, the form is designed to open a pre-filled email draft on your device for you to review and send yourself.

3. Service providers

HanyaGo relies on third-party services for website hosting and deployment, authentication and database functions, and externally hosted transport photographs. If CAPTCHA protection is used, the CAPTCHA provider processes the information required to perform its security check. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms.

4. Browser storage

The site can save your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. The authentication client may use browser storage to maintain your signed-in session. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, though clearing session data may sign you out or reset your theme choice.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be made available to authorised personnel or a relevant transport operator only when needed to review and fulfil a journey request, and to service providers that operate the website and database. We do not need your password or one-time verification code to help with a booking.

6. Retention and security

Account and booking records may be retained while the account or relevant service records remain active, or for legitimate operational and legal needs. HanyaGo uses database access controls and authentication protections, but no online service can promise absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

Keep your profile details accurate, use your account to review your own booking information where that feature is available, and contact HanyaGo to ask about access or correction of information. The website does not yet provide a complete self-service account deletion workflow.

8. Children and sensitive information

The website is not designed to collect sensitive personal information through a booking form. A parent or guardian should supervise any use by a minor. Do not send identity documents, financial account details, passwords or verification codes through a support message unless a verified, secure workflow is specifically provided.

9. Updates to this notice

This policy may change as HanyaGo adds booking management, notifications, online payments or other capabilities. The updated date above indicates the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Use the Contact page to reach HanyaGo once an official support channel is configured.

Launch note: Before HanyaGo accepts real passengers at commercial scale, the operator should confirm a monitored privacy contact, retention policy, data-controller details and any additional legal disclosures required for its operating locations.
