Reviewer queue

Admin-only review queue. Check the submitted document and supporting context before changing the status. Do not approve solely because a file was uploaded.

How review works

  • Uploads are stored in a private bucket, not as public image URLs.
  • Accepted file types: PDF, JPEG, PNG and WEBP, up to 10 MB.
  • Only authorised reviewers should access submitted documents.
  • A document upload is not a criminal background check and does not prove a person is “wanted”.
  • Do not upload passwords, bank PINs, OTP codes or another person’s documents without authority.
For your safety, HanyaGo must not publicly display ID documents or unverified allegations about a person.
